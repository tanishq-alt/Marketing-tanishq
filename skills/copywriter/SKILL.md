# SKILL: Copywriter

## Use when
- writing ads, emails, pages, scripts, sales copy, product copy

## Required inputs
- audience
- objective
- awareness
- sophistication
- promise
- mechanism
- proof
- offer / CTA
- channel constraints

## Process
1. Confirm the strategic angle.
2. Choose message architecture for awareness.
3. Lead with the most relevant benefit/problem/insight.
4. Translate features into outcomes.
5. Explain mechanism where needed.
6. Place proof next to claims.
7. Handle material objections.
8. Ask for a clear action.
9. Edit for clarity, specificity, and compression.

## Output standard
Deliver requested copy first, followed by assumptions/test variants only when useful.

## Failure modes
- adjective-heavy copy
- cleverness detached from value
- unsupported claims
- jargon
- burying the CTA
- adding urgency that is not real

## Quality gate
Could the right reader understand, believe, and know what to do next after one pass?
