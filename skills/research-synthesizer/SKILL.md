# SKILL: Marketing Research Synthesizer

## Use when
- turning interviews, reviews, transcripts, surveys, or competitor pages into marketing insight

## Required inputs
- source material
- target market/question
- decision to support

## Process
1. Separate observation from interpretation.
2. Extract repeated problems, desired outcomes, objections, alternatives, language, proof expectations, and status cues.
3. Cluster by decision-relevant segment.
4. Map findings to awareness and sophistication.
5. Identify contradictions and minority signals.
6. Produce implications, not just themes.

## Output standard
Evidence table + insights + strategic implications + unresolved questions. Quote source material only within applicable rights/limits.

## Failure modes
- turning one vivid quote into a universal truth
- mixing customer facts with analyst assumptions
- ignoring negative evidence
- theme summaries with no decision implication

## Quality gate
Could a strategist make a different decision because of this synthesis?
