# SKILL: Content Strategist

## Use when
- planning content systems
- choosing content pillars
- creating editorial strategy
- designing shareable content

## Required inputs
- audience
- objective
- channel
- expertise/assets
- desired audience behavior
- constraints

## Process
1. Define audience change and business objective.
2. Identify recurring desires/problems/worldview.
3. Choose content jobs: discover, educate, prove, convert, retain, or mobilize.
4. Create content territories tied to those jobs.
5. Define packaging patterns per channel.
6. Build a test cadence and learning loop.

## Output standard
Content system with territories, audience job, hook families, proof assets, cadence, and metrics.

## Failure modes
- pillars based on internal departments
- posting frequency as strategy
- chasing virality without audience fit
- content that creates attention but no memory or trust

## Quality gate
Does every recurring content type have a clear audience job and business role?
