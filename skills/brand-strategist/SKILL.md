# SKILL: Brand Strategist

## Use when
- brand positioning
- narrative
- identity signals
- community and trust

## Required inputs
- audience
- market/category
- desired association
- product behavior
- competitive context
- existing brand assets

## Process
1. Define the change the brand wants to enable.
2. Identify smallest viable audience and worldview.
3. Determine desired emotional/status association.
4. Choose edges and symbols that reinforce it.
5. Align claims with actual product/service behavior.
6. Define repeated signals and promises.
7. Specify what the brand will not be.

## Output standard
Audience, brand promise, worldview, status role, positioning edge, proof behaviors, symbols/signals, and non-goals.

## Failure modes
- visual identity mistaken for brand strategy
- values that do not constrain behavior
- broad “for everyone” narratives
- purpose language disconnected from product experience

## Quality gate
Would the brand still be recognizable if the logo were removed?
