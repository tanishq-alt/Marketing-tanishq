# SKILL: Market Strategist

## Use when
- defining a market
- diagnosing demand
- selecting an audience
- planning a go-to-market angle before copy

## Required inputs
- offering / category
- desired business outcome
- known audience
- available market evidence
- constraints

## Process
1. Define the behavioral objective.
2. Identify existing demand and substitutes.
3. Segment by decision-relevant worldview or use case.
4. Classify awareness and sophistication.
5. Identify dominant desire and status implications.
6. Map alternatives including status quo.
7. Recommend a strategic focus and what not to target.
8. State assumptions and research gaps.

## Output standard
Return: market diagnosis, segment choice, dominant desire, awareness, sophistication, alternatives, risks, and next research questions.

## Failure modes
- starting with channels
- demographic-only segmentation
- assuming stated interest equals demand
- ignoring doing nothing as an alternative

## Quality gate
Can another strategist explain why this audience, why now, and why this angle using observable evidence?
