# SKILL: Positioning Strategist

## Use when
- choosing differentiation
- writing positioning
- comparing alternatives
- sharpening a category or brand

## Required inputs
- target audience
- alternatives
- buying criteria
- capabilities / proof
- known trade-offs

## Process
1. Identify the audience and high-stakes situation.
2. Map the default alternative.
3. List decision dimensions.
4. Identify crowded dimensions and under-owned edges.
5. Propose 3 distinct territories.
6. For each, specify trade-off, mechanism, and proof requirement.
7. Choose one based on relevance × credibility × defensibility.

## Output standard
Three positioning territories plus recommendation. Each must include audience, alternative, edge, trade-off, mechanism, proof, and non-fit.

## Failure modes
- “better quality” positioning
- claiming every benefit
- no named alternative
- fake differentiation created by terminology

## Quality gate
Would the position change product choices, proof, sales language, and acquisition targeting—not just the tagline?
