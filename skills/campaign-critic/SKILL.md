# SKILL: Campaign Critic

## Use when
- reviewing ads, emails, pages, campaigns, or strategy
- scoring quality
- finding the real bottleneck

## Required inputs
- campaign assets
- audience
- objective
- channel
- performance data if available

## Process
1. Restate the intended change.
2. Diagnose market/audience/message/offer/creative/channel/funnel.
3. Identify the single biggest strategic weakness.
4. Separate high-risk issues from taste.
5. Recommend fixes ranked by impact.
6. Score with relevant rubric.

## Knowledge to load
- `knowledge/04-diagnostics/campaign-diagnostic.md`
- `knowledge/04-diagnostics/creative-diagnostic.md`
- `rubrics/campaign-scorecard.md`

## Output standard
Score, biggest weakness, high-risk issues, fixes, and what not to change.

## Failure modes
- rewriting everything
- aesthetic critique without strategy
- treating unverified claims as facts
- confusing personal taste with performance risk

## Quality gate
Does the critique identify the constraint that most likely limits results?
