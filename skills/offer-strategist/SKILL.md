# SKILL: Offer Strategist

## Use when
- packaging an offer
- improving conversion
- pricing/terms communication
- reducing buyer risk

## Required inputs
- product/service
- target audience
- desired outcome
- price/terms
- delivery constraints
- objections

## Process
1. Identify desired outcome and urgency.
2. Map buyer effort, risk, and time-to-value.
3. Compare current alternative.
4. Improve value before adding persuasion.
5. Clarify scope, terms, support, and next step.
6. Add legitimate risk reducers.
7. Check that urgency/scarcity is factual.

## Knowledge to load
- `knowledge/01-strategy/offer-and-value.md`
- `knowledge/01-strategy/pricing-as-signal.md`
- `knowledge/02-copy/objection-reframing.md`

## Output standard
Offer architecture, value narrative, objections, risk reducers, CTA, and test ideas.

## Failure modes
- compensating for weak value with bonuses
- fake scarcity
- hiding material terms
- increasing promise without increasing proof

## Quality gate
Is the offer itself stronger, or did we merely make the language more aggressive?
