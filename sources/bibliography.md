1. Eugene M. Schwartz — *Breakthrough Advertising*.
2. Robert W. Bly — *The Copywriter's Handbook*.
3. Seth Godin — *This Is Marketing: You Can't Be Seen Until You Learn to See*.
4. Brendan Kane — *Hook Point: How to Stand Out in a 3-Second World*.

## Important provenance note

The working documents supplied during construction were not equally complete:

- *Breakthrough Advertising*: full/near-full book text was available.
- *This Is Marketing*: full/near-full book text was available.
- *The Copywriter's Handbook*: the supplied PDF was a short summary/condensation, not the full book.
- *Hook Point*: the supplied PDF was a Bookey-produced summary and commentary document, not the original book.

Accordingly, this repository treats the latter two as lower-confidence secondary representations where exact author intent matters.
