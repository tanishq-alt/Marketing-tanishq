# Storytelling for Marketing

Story is useful when it compresses causality, tension, proof, and identity into a sequence humans can follow.

## Minimal marketing story

1. **Context** — who/where are we?
2. **Tension** — what is wrong, uncertain, risky, or desired?
3. **Attempt** — what was tried?
4. **Insight / mechanism** — what changed the understanding?
5. **Action** — what was done differently?
6. **Result** — what changed?
7. **Meaning** — why should this audience care?

## Customer-centered story

The brand does not need to be the hero. Often it is more credible as:

- guide;
- tool;
- proof source;
- system;
- enabler;
- antagonist to an old constraint.

## Story selection

Choose stories that prove the strategic claim. A moving story with no connection to the purchase decision is entertainment, not necessarily marketing.

## Meeting / sales story rule

Prepare stories, but listen first. The best story is the one that resolves the prospect's actual tension, not the one the seller most wants to tell.
