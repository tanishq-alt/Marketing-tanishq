# Landing Page Diagnostic

Score the page on seven jobs:

1. **Orientation** — Do I know what this is?
2. **Relevance** — Is it obviously for me / my situation?
3. **Value** — Do I understand what improves?
4. **Difference** — Why this instead of alternatives?
5. **Belief** — Why should I trust the claims?
6. **Risk reduction** — Are important objections handled?
7. **Action** — Is the next step clear and proportionate?

## Section order by audience

Do not use one universal layout.

- High awareness → offer and proof can appear early.
- Low awareness → problem/context may need to precede product detail.
- High sophistication → mechanism/difference should appear earlier.
- High risk → proof, security, process, and objections require more space.

## Red flags

- hero headline is a category description with no outcome;
- benefit claims without proof;
- feature grid before the audience understands value;
- testimonials that do not support the main claim;
- CTA asks for more commitment than the page earned;
- multiple competing primary CTAs;
- message mismatch with the ad/email that drove the visit.
