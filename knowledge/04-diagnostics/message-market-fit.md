# Message-Market Fit

Message-market fit exists when the intended audience quickly recognizes:

- this is about a problem/desire I care about;
- this offer is meaningfully different or well-suited to me;
- the promise is believable enough to investigate;
- the language sounds like it understands my situation.

## Signals of weak fit

- high impressions, low qualified attention;
- clicks driven by curiosity but weak conversion;
- repeated “what is this?” questions;
- buyers consistently care about a different benefit than the one marketed;
- sales calls spend most of their time correcting the marketing frame;
- strong conversion only after heavy explanation.

## Improvement loop

1. Collect real customer language.
2. Identify repeated desired outcomes.
3. Compare them with current messaging.
4. Classify awareness/sophistication.
5. Rewrite strategic angle, not just wording.
6. Test on a narrow segment.
7. Feed sales/customer response back into positioning.
