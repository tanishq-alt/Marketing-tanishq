# Creative Diagnostic

When creative is weak, identify which job failed.

## Failure taxonomy

- **Stop failure** — audience never notices it.
- **Selection failure** — wrong people stop.
- **Promise failure** — people do not understand the payoff.
- **Relevance failure** — payoff is understood but not important.
- **Belief failure** — promise feels implausible.
- **Retention failure** — opening works, middle drags.
- **Payoff failure** — content does not deliver what opening promised.
- **Action failure** — audience consumes but does not know what to do next.

## Creative review questions

- What single thing should the first frame communicate?
- Is the visual doing persuasive work or merely decorating?
- Is there unnecessary setup?
- Does proof appear before skepticism peaks?
- Does the CTA match the level of intent created?
