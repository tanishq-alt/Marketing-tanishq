# Campaign Diagnostic

Use this when a campaign underperforms.

## Diagnose by layer

### 1. Market
- Is there enough real desire?
- Is timing wrong?

### 2. Audience
- Are we reaching the people with the problem and ability to act?

### 3. Positioning
- Does the offering occupy a clear, relevant edge?

### 4. Message
- Does it fit awareness and sophistication?

### 5. Belief
- Are claims supported?

### 6. Offer
- Is the exchange attractive enough?

### 7. Creative
- Does the packaging earn attention from the right audience?

### 8. Channel
- Is the format native to the medium?

### 9. Funnel
- Does post-click experience match the promise?

### 10. Measurement
- Are we optimizing a proxy that hides poor customer quality?

## Rule

Do not rewrite the ad until you know which layer is most likely broken.
