# Funnel and Friction

A funnel is a sequence of commitments under changing levels of attention, trust, motivation, and friction.

## Six leak types

1. **Wrong traffic** — the people entering are poor-fit.
2. **Promise mismatch** — acquisition message and destination do not align.
3. **Comprehension friction** — people do not understand the offer.
4. **Belief friction** — people understand but doubt the claim.
5. **Action friction** — too many steps, fields, choices, or commitments.
6. **Post-action friction** — onboarding or delivery breaks the promise.

## Funnel audit

At each step, record:

- audience state;
- promise;
- required belief;
- requested action;
- friction;
- evidence available;
- measurable conversion event.

## Optimization order

Fix in this order unless evidence says otherwise:

1. traffic quality;
2. promise-message match;
3. clarity;
4. belief;
5. value/offer;
6. friction;
7. micro-copy.

Button-color optimization on a broken promise is theater.
