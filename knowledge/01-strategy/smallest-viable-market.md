# Smallest Viable Market

The smallest viable market is the narrowest audience that can sustain the objective while allowing unusually strong relevance and service.

## Why it works

Trying to appeal to everyone pushes messaging toward averages. A smaller, coherent audience gives you sharper language, clearer trade-offs, stronger product fit, and better word of mouth.

## How to define it

Choose a segment where members share:

- a meaningful problem or desire;
- similar decision criteria;
- reachable distribution channels;
- sufficient willingness/ability to act;
- enough common language to support a specific promise.

## Test

A useful smallest viable market should let you finish this sentence:

> For people who ______ and care deeply about ______, this is the better choice because ______.

If the sentence applies to nearly everyone, the segment is not sharp enough.

## Expansion rule

Win one coherent segment, learn why it works, then expand to adjacent groups that share the same underlying motive—not merely demographic similarity.
