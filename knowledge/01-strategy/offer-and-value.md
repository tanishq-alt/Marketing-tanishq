# Offer and Value

An offer is the complete exchange, not just the product or price.

## Offer components

- outcome;
- product/service scope;
- speed/time-to-value;
- effort required from buyer;
- price;
- payment structure;
- onboarding/setup;
- support;
- guarantees or risk reversal when legitimate;
- bonuses/add-ons;
- availability constraints;
- CTA and next step.

## Value diagnosis

A weak offer may fail because:

1. the outcome is not important;
2. the outcome is important but not believable;
3. the effort feels too high;
4. the risk feels too high;
5. the time-to-value is too long;
6. the comparison set makes the price unattractive;
7. the buyer cannot justify the decision socially or financially;
8. the next step is too large.

## Value communication sequence

1. Name the desired change.
2. Show the mechanism.
3. Translate capabilities into outcomes.
4. Prove the important claims.
5. Expose relevant trade-offs honestly.
6. Reduce avoidable friction.
7. Ask for a proportionate next action.
