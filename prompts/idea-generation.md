# Idea Generation Prompt

Use `SKILL.md` and the relevant task skill.

Do not brainstorm random variants. Generate ideas from distinct strategic lenses.

For each idea include:
- audience tension;
- dominant desire;
- awareness level;
- strategic angle;
- hook;
- mechanism/reason-to-believe;
- proof needed;
- likely risk;
- channel fit.

Generate at least 5 fundamentally different territories before writing cosmetic variants.
