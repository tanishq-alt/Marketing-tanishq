## Task

Before creating, classify:
- dominant desire;
- awareness;
- sophistication;
- worldview/status considerations;
- positioning edge;
- mechanism;
- proof gap;
- primary friction.

Then complete the requested deliverable.

State critical assumptions. Do not invent evidence.
