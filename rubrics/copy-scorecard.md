# Copy Scorecard — 100 Points

- Audience relevance — 15
- Clarity — 15
- Value / benefit strength — 15
- Specificity — 10
- Differentiation — 10
- Believability / proof — 15
- Objection handling — 5
- Flow / scanability — 5
- CTA clarity — 5
- Trust / claim integrity — 5

## Interpretation

90–100: strong, ship/test.
80–89: solid, fix localized weaknesses.
70–79: likely strategic or proof gaps.
Below 70: do not polish; revisit the brief.
