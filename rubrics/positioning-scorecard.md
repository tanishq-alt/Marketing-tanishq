# Positioning Scorecard — 50 Points

Score 1–5:

1. Audience specificity
2. Problem importance
3. Alternative clarity
4. Edge relevance
5. Edge distinctiveness
6. Credibility
7. Defensibility
8. Trade-off clarity
9. Product alignment
10. Ability to guide execution

A position that cannot guide product, proof, sales, and creative choices is too abstract.
