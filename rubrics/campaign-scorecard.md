# Campaign Scorecard — 100 Points

- Market / timing — 10
- Audience selection — 10
- Desire / problem strength — 10
- Awareness fit — 10
- Sophistication fit — 10
- Positioning / differentiation — 10
- Offer — 10
- Proof / trust — 10
- Creative / packaging — 10
- Funnel / measurement — 10

## Hard fails

Regardless of score, flag as high risk if the campaign depends on:

- fabricated proof;
- misleading urgency;
- materially false competitor claims;
- bait-and-switch hooks;
- impossible guarantees;
- targeting that conflicts with legal or platform requirements.
