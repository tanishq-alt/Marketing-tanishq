# Worked Example: Creator / Newsletter

## Scenario
A creator wants to grow a newsletter about operating a small professional-services firm.

## Diagnosis

- Audience: owners with 2–20 employees, not “entrepreneurs.”
- Desire: more control, fewer operational surprises, credible systems.
- Awareness: problem-aware to solution-aware.
- Worldview: skeptical of startup hype; values practical experience.
- Status: competent operator, not internet celebrity.

## Content strategy

Content jobs:
- diagnose common operating mistakes;
- show reusable systems;
- provide teardown examples;
- create language readers can share with their teams.

## Hook families

- diagnosis: “The meeting isn’t the problem. The missing decision rule is.”
- counterintuitive: “Your SOP library can make the company harder to run.”
- utility: “A 5-line handoff template for work that keeps bouncing back.”

The creator earns permission by repeatedly delivering usable insights, not by maximizing generic reach.
