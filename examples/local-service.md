# Worked Example: Local Service

## Scenario
A residential electrician wants more high-value jobs without competing on lowest price.

## Diagnosis

- Desire: safety, speed, confidence, minimal disruption.
- Awareness: most prospects are problem-aware when something fails.
- Alternative: another electrician, handyman, delay, DIY.
- Trust burden: high because the service enters the home and technical quality is hard to judge.

## Positioning edge

“Diagnosis you can see before you approve the repair.”

Mechanism:
- photo/video evidence;
- written options;
- fixed decision points;
- documented completion.

## Messaging

Lead with transparency and risk reduction, not “quality service.”

## Proof

- sample report;
- process photos;
- licensing/insurance where relevant;
- specific reviews describing communication and cleanliness.
