# Worked Example: E-commerce Product

## Scenario
A premium insulated bottle enters a crowded market.

## Diagnosis

- Desire: cold drinks, convenience, aesthetic identity.
- Awareness: product-aware category; many known alternatives.
- Sophistication: high; “keeps drinks cold for X hours” is commoditized.
- Status: design and affiliation may matter as much as insulation.

## Possible positioning edges

1. **Repairable for life** — durability + anti-disposable worldview.
2. **Fits specific carry systems** — practical compatibility edge.
3. **Quiet-use design** — lid and handling optimized for office/studio use.

Each requires product truth and proof. The marketer should not invent a mechanism just to sound different.

## Landing page implication

Lead with the chosen edge, demonstrate it visually, then support with expected category basics. Do not make “stainless steel” the hero unless the material itself explains a meaningful difference.
